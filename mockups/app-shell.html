Companion Overview

A working sketch of the app frame: sections in the rail, a page in the middle, an inspector on the right, and one story-time cursor that every age and every grid in the app follows. Operate everything below. Nothing is a screenshot.

All names, dates, and events here are example data from the first bible, East Winds. The app itself holds any novel.

One search box for the whole bible

Type in English or Chinese. Results come from characters, chapters, events, glossary, and places, filtered by section and status. Pick a result and the frame below jumps to it.

Every section lives in the left rail. The inspector on the right opens whatever you select without leaving the page you are on. The status bar carries the story time and where you stopped last session.

Outline: chapters against years

One row per chapter in reading order, grouped by part. Each chapter has two strands: a present strand (solid mark) and a backstory strand (hollow mark). Arcs run as bands in the family color of the character who owns them. The orange line is the story time. Flip the order and the chiasm becomes a chronology.

Editing rules that apply everywhere

Autosave within a second, fifty steps of undo, a trash instead of a delete, and a link picker on the @ key. The demo below runs on the same character data as the frame, so an undo here changes the ages up there.
