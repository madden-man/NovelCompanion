Novel Companion after A39

A working model of the proposed app: the eight screens on the rail, the top bar, and Relevant to Session. Every control changes what you see. The people, chapters and events are East Winds names with example dates and example world events, not the bible's own figures.

Try: drag a person in the Family tree onto another. Drag a chapter bar's edge in the Outline. Click an empty cell in World events. Pick a character in Backstory and edit a row. Open Relevant and expand a tile.
