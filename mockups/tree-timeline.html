Family Tree and Timeline

Every age and every alive-or-dead answer on this page is computed by ordinary code from birth and death years, never by a model. The data is the East Winds bible, the first project in the app; everything below works for any novel.

Story time: 2056. The year control in the tree, the year view, the timeline cursor, and the compare table all follow it.
