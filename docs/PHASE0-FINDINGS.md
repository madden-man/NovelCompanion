## World

- Premise: China rises through factory automation; the US declines after the 2039 collapse of the Mississippi and Colorado basins, in two cycles of crash and partial recovery.
- Timeline: about 250 dated entries 2025 to 2100 in the High Level sheet under 90 category rows in ten groups: Geopolitics, Pressure Gauges, Technology, China Industrial Capacity, Financial Systems, Military Dominance, Resources, Demographics, Foreign Relations, US Domestic Policy, Cultural Shifts, Second Order Effects.
- Eras in the prose doc: 2025 to 2038 (the automation divide), 2039 (the hinge), 2040 to 2049 (first rally), 2050 to 2062 (second descent), 2063 to 2066 (second crash), 2066 to 2072 (second rally), 2073 to 2075 (Pacific States Compact and the Port of Los Angeles standoff), 2076 to 2089 (the Compact matures), 2090 to 2100 (associate membership).
- Key dates the family story leans on: 2035 Japan folds into China's supply chain and Frank's CIA work; 2039 water crisis (Dawn's birth year in the older sheets); 2048 Pacific Coordination Framework; 2052 Unified Credit; 2053 Taiwan joins PCF; 2055 reverse brain drain while Qin still sends Dawn to America; 2063 US depression; 2066 third-force coalition and Mark's wedding; 2075 port standoff; 2090 Long Beach plot stopped by autonomous port security (HaoYu's drone death is 2093); 2100 associate membership.
- Real research list: How China Escaped the Poverty Trap, Forbidden Nation, Forgotten Ally, Brothers, Life and Death Are Wearing Me Out, The Three-Body Problem, Wild Swans, Demolition Man.

## Plot structure

Chiasm form. Book One: Part One "Sins of the Father" (Mark), Part Two "Lost Spirits" (Dawn), Part Three "Inheritance" (Qin and Frank, ending with old Mark), Part Four "Circulation" (Siyuan). Book Two "I Take Refuge in You": Parts "Claws Come Out", "Fangs of the Dragon", "What Ties Us Together", "Tears Us Apart". Twelve chapter titles per Book One part exist (Part One has two chapters numbered 8 and no 9). Each chapter carries two dated strands, a backstory year and a present arc year, for example Part One Ch. 1 "Mark Feels Free" is backstory 2051 plus "Mark: College Arc" 2055; Part Four Ch. 10 "O Death" is "Siyuan: Lost Years" 2074 plus "Forestory: John" 2096. Plot v2 restarts Part One with two scenes.
