# A47 grids brief: the grids are drawable (IP-4, Moments of IP-13)

Read `docs/briefs/A47-common.md` first. Wave 0 has merged: `Editable` exists (API below your brief), the bible's numbers (`timelineStart`, `timelineEnd`, `windowBefore`, `windowAfter`, `pickerReach`) are in settings and `worldGrid.ts` and `backstoryGrid.ts` take them as parameters. You own `src/renderer/src/workspace/timeline/WorldEventsGrid.tsx`, `TimelineView.tsx`, `gridShared.tsx`, `timeline/*.css`, `src/renderer/src/workspace/backstory/*`, `src/shared/domain/worldGrid.ts` and `backstoryGrid.ts` (the hit and drag logic for ends and edges), `tests/e2e/timeline.spec.ts`, `tests/e2e/backstory.spec.ts`, `tests/unit/backstory-grid.test.ts`, `tests/unit/world-grid.test.ts` (or whatever the grid's unit test is called). Branch `a47-grids`.

Deliverables:

1. **Lifespan ends drag** (Backstory). A lifespan bar's left end sets birth and its right end sets death: a grab handle at each end (visible on hover and focus, 8px wide, keyboard reachable), drag snaps to the year, release writes the character's `birth` or `death` through the entry write path as one step ("Birth of <name>"); Shift with the arrows on the focused bar moves the nearest end by a year (Shift+Left and Shift+Right for the left end, Shift+Alt for the right, or a clearer scheme you document in the tooltip). A death dragged before the birth is refused at release with the ED-10 explanation and the bar snaps back.
2. **Era band edges drag** (both grids). The era band's segments get the same handles; dragging an edge writes the era's `startYear` or `endYear`; when two eras meet, dragging the shared edge moves both ends together (one step, "Moved the edge between <a> and <b>"). Double-click (or Enter on the focused gap) on a gap in the band creates an era over the gap (name from `templates.eraName`) and opens its name as an `Editable` field on the band; the name is `Editable` on every segment (double-click or F2).
3. **Marker labels rename** (both grids). Double-click (or F2) on a marker's label opens the title as an `Editable` text field in place; Enter saves as one step. The single click still opens the editor under the grid (World events) or picks the person (Backstory).
4. **Backstory rows.** The character name row is a link to the person's page (a click on the name opens it; a click elsewhere on the row still picks the person). In the moments table a moment can move to another person through an `Editable` select on the row ("Whose moment"), one step; the grid redraws. "Pick events" in the world context inserts a link to the event (an `@Title` mention per ED-9) rather than plain text, and its reach is `pickerReach` from settings.
5. **IP-13 Moments.** When no person is picked, the Backstory screen shows every moment as the table (sorted by year, then person), with the same in-place cells and a New moment that asks for the person; picking a person filters it as today.
6. **Numbers.** The axis spans `timelineStart` to `timelineEnd`, the world-around window uses `windowBefore` and `windowAfter`, and the grid zoom and labels follow (no hardcoded 2025 or 2100 left in your files; grep).
7. **Tests**: unit tests for the hit logic of ends and edges and the snap; e2e rows prefixed with the criterion: a lifespan end drags and writes birth as one step and Undo restores it, Shift+arrows do the same, a death before birth is refused with the reason, an era edge drags, a shared edge moves both, a gap double-click makes an era named from the template, a label renames on F2, the name row opens the page, a moment moves to another person, Pick events inserts a mention, every moment shows when no person is picked, the axis follows the settings' span, NF-2 by keyboard in both themes.
