# A39 model brief: the cut model, migration 9, the character page (SM-1 to SM-4)

Read `docs/briefs/A39-common.md` first. You own the files listed there under `a39-model`. SPEC: SM-1, SM-2, SM-3, SM-4, SM-12, plus `chapters.sceneNote` from SM-10. Section 7 (fixture novel) and section 8 (test plan) still bind.

Tommy's words: "the data structure is far too complicated ... a character, for example, should have 1) name, 2) chinese name where applicable, 3) short description, 4) description, 5) backstory where applicable", then "drop facts and knowledge records, drop themes, drop custom sections, drop arcs in favor of backstory grid, yes backstory is per character, drop relationships entirely".

Work in this order, committing after each step, so the tree compiles at every commit:

1. **Schema.** `src/shared/schema/character.ts` keeps `role`, `names.full`, `names.chinese`, `birth`, `death`, `familyIds`, `backstory` and the common fields; everything else in SM-1 goes, including `Names.order` (treat every name as written). Add `src/shared/schema/moment.ts` exactly as the common brief gives it, with its `FieldSpec`s, registered in `entrySchemas` and `src/shared/schema/index.ts` (kind label "Moment", plural "Moments", warnings: a moment outside the character's lifetime, like the old voice note warning). World events lose `reality` and `placeIds`; era states lose the five prose fields; cultural entries lose `generationNotes`; chapters lose `arcIds` and `factsInPlay` and gain `sceneNote`. Remove the schemas, field specs, kind labels and manifest rows for `facts`, `knowledgeRecords`, `themes`, `customSections`, `customEntries`, `arcs`, `relationships`, `plotBeats`. Search filters (`searchFilters.ts`), the IPC contract, the Claude tools and prompts (`src/main/claude/tools.ts`, `prompts.ts`, every feature), the read model, the search index, the JSON backup and the Drive Markdown export all follow the schema. A JSON export written by an older app (which still holds the dropped collections) must still import: the importer in `src/main/backup/json.ts` migrates it through migration 9 like a bible, and a test proves a version 8 export with facts and beats comes in as moments.
2. **Migration 9** (`src/main/db/migrations/009-simplified-model.ts`, `LATEST_SCHEMA_VERSION` becomes 9), per bible, in one pass: make a moment from every live plot beat (`characterId` from `povCharacterId`, `year`, `title`, `body` from `experienceNote`, `worldContext`, status, provenance, source note, tags, created and modified times carried over), from every voice note (year as given, or birth year plus the age; `title` "Voice", `body` the note text; skip when the character's birth is unknown and the note is an age, and record the skip in the migration's summary), and from every place lived (`title` "Lives in <place>", `year` from `startYear`, `endYear` from `endYear`; skip when `startYear` is null). Beats and voice notes and places in the trash become moments in the trash. Then unset the dropped character fields, `reality`, `placeIds`, the era prose fields, `generationNotes`, `arcIds` and `factsInPlay` with `$unset`, and drop the eight collections and their revisions. Write one revision-free summary document to `meta` (`migration9`: counts made, skipped and dropped, taken at) so the architect can check the East Winds numbers in the audit (134 beats, 9 voice notes, 21 places lived) against it. Indexes on `moments`: `{ characterId: 1, year: 1 }`.
3. **Snapshot before the drop (SM-2).** `openBibleSession` in `src/main/db/session.ts` takes a snapshot (a new `SnapshotReason` `'migration'`, label "Before migration 9") before running migrations whenever `snapshotRoot` is given and the bible's version is below 9. Build the `SnapshotService` before `migrate`; the keep count can be a constant for that one call. The read-only path stays as it is. An integration test opens a version 8 bible with facts, beats and voice notes, asserts the snapshot exists and holds every dropped document, and that the bible then has the expected moments and no dropped collection. Restoring that snapshot (`src/main/backup/restore.ts`) must still work: it replaces every collection and then re-migrates, which runs migration 9 again. Prove it.
4. **Fixture novel.** `fixtures/novel/`: delete `facts.json`, `knowledgeRecords.json`, `customSections.json`, `customEntries.json`; strip the dropped fields from `characters.json`, `worldEvents.json`, `eraStates.json`, `culturalEntries.json`, `chapters.json`; add `moments.json` with the beats, voice notes and places the old fixture carried (the same moments migration 9 would make) so section 7's known answers stay true. `fixtures/expected/sb2-selection.json` and `structural-findings.json` change where the brief and the continuity check no longer read facts, voice notes or marks: regenerate them, read the diff by hand, and list in your report every expected answer that changed and why it is right. `tl2.json` should not change.
5. **Engines.** The brief (`src/shared/domain/brief/selection.ts`, `render.ts`, `src/main/claude/features/briefs.ts`) reads the character's moments up to the story year instead of voice notes, places lived and backstory marks; the "voice" of a character is the moments titled "Voice" (keep SB-3's intent: how they speak at that age). Relevant to Session's backstory (`sessions/relevantBody.tsx`, `ipcSession.ts`) lists moments instead of marks. The cultural-context engine (A35) reads where a character lives from "Lives in" moments. The continuity check loses the facts step (`continuity/*`, `findings.ts` kinds) and keeps ages, deaths and places. Capture (`features/capture.ts`) files to moments instead of voice notes, and never to a dropped kind. The question panel's tools lose the dropped kinds.
6. **Character page (SM-1).** `character.tsx`: name and Chinese name as the heading (`Zh` hover meanings stay), then one line with birth, death, status and family chips and the age at the story year, then the relatives as a read-only line that opens the tree (parents, partners with the union years, children), then three text boxes that grow with their text: In the story (`role`), Description (`body`), Backstory (`backstory`). No carets, no other fields. The `NewEntryForm` for a character asks for the full name only. The generic editor (`editor.tsx`, `fields.tsx`) still renders every other kind. Delete `knowledge.tsx`, `outline/Arcs.tsx` and the arcs panel in `OutlineView.tsx` (fewest lines there; the outline builder owns the rest of that file), the arcs, themes and facts-in-play fields of `ChapterPage.tsx`, the dropped kinds in `entries/EntriesView.tsx` (the screen itself leaves the rail in wave 2), and the rail buttons of dropped sections in `Workspace.tsx` (fewest lines). Remove the dropped kinds from `importProposals.tsx` and `src/main/claude/features/importProposals.ts`.
7. **Tests.** Unit: moment schema and warnings; migration 9 mapping as pure functions in `src/shared/domain/` (beat to moment, voice note to moment with and without a birth year, place to moment), tested on the audit's cases; brief selection over moments; continuity without facts. Integration: migration 9 on a seeded version 8 bible (counts, trash carried, unset fields, dropped collections gone, the `meta` summary), the snapshot before it, restore after it, JSON import of an old export. E2e (`editing.spec.ts`, `knowledge.spec.ts`, `session.spec.ts`, `keyboard.spec.ts`, `tree.spec.ts`, `capture.spec.ts`, `backup.spec.ts` and whichever else you touch): the character page shows exactly the SM-1 layout and saves each box, the relatives line opens the tree, NF-2 keyboard rows for the screens you changed, capture files a moment. Delete the tests of withdrawn criteria (CH-2, KN, TH, CS, PL-1 to PL-4 where they test arcs and beats, A9, A35 marks) and list them. `tests/e2e/plot.spec.ts` and `timeline.spec.ts` belong to the other builders: if your change breaks a test there, note the test and the reason in your report instead of editing it.

Numbers to check against: East Winds today has 32 characters (9 with voice notes, 21 with places lived), 134 plot beats, 35 arcs, 23 relationships, 32 facts, 26 knowledge records, 16 themes, 1 custom section with 2 entries. Migration 9 on that bible should make about 134 beat moments, up to 9 voice moments and up to 21 place moments.
