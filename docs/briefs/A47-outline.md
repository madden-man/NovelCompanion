# A47 outline brief: strand popovers on the bars, the Story time dialog, scene typing undo (IP-3, IP-5, the outline side of IP-6, Books and Parts of IP-13)

Read `docs/briefs/A47-common.md` first. Wave 0 has merged: `Editable` exists (API below your brief), templates and numbers are in settings, lists carry colour. You own `src/renderer/src/workspace/outline/*`, `timeline/StoryTimeDialog.tsx`, `timeline/LivedThrough.tsx`, `timeline/CompareView.tsx`, `src/shared/domain/sceneOutline.ts`, `src/shared/domain/timeline.ts` only where the dialog's rows need an ID they do not carry, `tests/e2e/plot.spec.ts`, `tests/e2e/year.spec.ts`, `tests/unit/scene-outline.test.ts`. Deliverables:

1. **IP-3 the bar's strand popover.** Each chapter bar shows a strand chip (the writing strand's label and year, in the primary text colour). Clicking it, or Enter on it, opens a popover anchored to the bar (`role="dialog"` is fine, but it is not modal: the outline behind it stays readable and scrollable, it closes on Escape, on a click outside, and on Tab leaving it, and the focus returns to the chip). Inside: the writing strand's label (`Editable` text), point of view (`Editable` select over the chapter's characters present), characters present (`Editable` chips with the character picker and "Add new..."), places (`Editable` chips with the places picker), and the backstory strand's year (`Editable` year; "no backstory strand" with "Add one" when there is none, which adds a strand labelled from `templates.strandBackstory`). Each is one undo step. The written flag is a toggle on the bar (a small check control, keyboard reachable, label "Written").
2. **IP-3 the first part.** The first part's title shows as a divider above the first chapter and renames there like every other divider (`Scenes.tsx` 1010 and 1196 to 1207); its removal is not offered when it is the only part.
3. **IP-6 scene typing.** Typing in the scene outline records one undo step per pause (the A44 rule: a step per autosave, Cmd+Z inside the box with unsaved typing undoes the typing first). Today typing goes to autosave with no step (`Scenes.tsx` 333 to 352 against 358 to 369). Build it so a structural move and a typing pause are distinct steps with plain labels ("Typed in the scene outline", "Moved a chapter").
4. **IP-13 Books and Parts** under the Outline: a disclosure under the track ("Books and parts") listing every book and part with its title as `Editable` text, part summary as the A44 box, and New part and New book, each one step. The part list is the dividers' source, so a rename here and on a divider agree.
5. **IP-5 the Story time dialog.** Each person's row: the name links to their page (closes the dialog, opens the character), birth and death are `Editable` date in the row (the state and age recompute at once). The era panel edits name, start, end (year fields) and body (the A44 box) in place. Each chapter strand in the year is a link that closes the dialog and opens the chapter's bar on the Outline, scrolled into view, with its popover closed. Lived through and Compare rows link the name to the person. The dialog is the year view Tommy opens mid-sentence: no field opening may move the rows (reserve the width).
6. **Templates**: "New chapter", "New section", "Part {n}", "Book {n}", strand labels and "New era" come from `templates` in settings wherever the outline or the dialog creates one.
7. **Tests**: `plot.spec.ts` rows prefixed with their criterion: the popover opens and closes without moving the outline (bounding boxes), each field in it writes the strand as one step, Add one adds a backstory strand, the written toggle, the first part's divider renames, scene typing is one step per pause and two across a pause, Books and parts list and rename, a template title is used for a new chapter; `year.spec.ts`: a row's name opens the page, birth edits in place and the age follows, the era panel edits in place, a strand row opens the chapter on the Outline, NF-2 by keyboard in both themes.
