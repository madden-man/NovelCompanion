# M4 brief: plot, culture, places, glossary, knowledge, themes, questions, log, custom sections, search

Criteria: PL-1 to PL-4, CU-1, CU-2, PC-1, GL-1 to GL-3, KN-1 to KN-3, TH-1, OQ-1, SL-1, CS-1 to CS-3, SR-1, SR-2. Read first: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `SPEC.md` sections 4, 5.5, 5.7, 5.8, 5.9, 5.14, 5.15, 6, 7, amendments A5 to A10 and A18, and the mockups `mockups/app-shell.html` (outline grid, glossary with hover meaning, open questions, search) and `mockups/brief-continuity.html` (knowledge states section). Then the code from M0 to M3; build on the generic editor, read model, search index, timeline and story time.

Deliverables, keeping `npm test` green:

1. **Plot** (PL-1 to PL-4): books, parts, chapters with strands, arcs with scope (book, family, character, custom) and owner and year range, beats with arcs (many), POV, year, world context and experience notes; a chapter or beat can belong to several arcs. Outline view as the chapter-by-year grid from the mockup: rows are chapters in reading order grouped by book and part, columns are years, strands drawn as marks (primary filled, backstory hollow), arcs as spans in the owner's family series color, a story-time cursor, a toggle to story-year order, click a row to open the chapter in the inspector where strand years are editable (PL-2). Drag to reorder chapters within a part and beats within an arc; reading order and story year stay separate fields (PL-3). The chapter page shows beats, characters present with ages in the strand's year, places, themes, facts in play (PL-4).

2. **Culture, places, glossary** (CU-1, CU-2, PC-1, GL-1 to GL-3): cultural entries by community (editable list) and year range, with `entriesFor(community, year)` as a pure function; places nest with a parent and hold descriptions by year range, `descriptionFor(place, year)`; glossary terms with characters, romanization, language, meaning, usage note; one romanization system per language in settings (Mandarin: Hanyu Pinyin; Cantonese: Jyutping; list editable) with a pure checker that flags terms not following the system (for Pinyin: only valid syllables with optional tone marks or tone numbers; implement a syllable table) (GL-1); search finds a term by characters, romanization with or without tone marks, or meaning (GL-2, extend the M0 tokenizer to strip tone marks); GL-3: a renderer component `Zh` that wraps any run of CJK characters, looks up the glossary (exact term first, then the longest matching prefix), shows romanization and meaning on hover and on keyboard focus, links character names to their term automatically, and offers "Add to glossary" for a run with no term; use it in character names, body text rendering, tree nodes, and list rows.

3. **Knowledge** (KN-1 to KN-3): facts with knowledge records (character, year learned, chapter learned, how); `whoKnows(fact)` and `knownBy(character, year)` pure functions; a fact page and a "what they know" panel on the character page; recording a fact learned before birth produces an ED-10 warning, never a block (KN-2); a chapter's "facts in play" editor: which characters act on which facts, stored on the chapter for CC-2 (KN-3).

4. **Themes, questions, log** (TH-1, OQ-1, SL-1): themes with appearances (chapter plus note); open questions linked to any entry with state open or decided and a decision text, filterable by state; session log entries with date, chapter, where I stopped, notes; closing the app (window close or Cmd+Q) prompts "Where did you stop?" with a text field that saves a log entry, and the next launch shows the latest note first on the dashboard placeholder (the dashboard proper arrives in M7; for now a launch panel) (SL-1).

5. **Custom sections** (CS-1 to CS-3): a section definition with fields of kind text, long text, number, year, year range, single choice (with options), tags, link to entry; custom entries carry the common entry fields, status, provenance, links, revisions, trash, and are searchable and importable and exportable (the JSON export in M5 must include them); a per-section brief rule: linked to the chapter, matching the story year, or always (stored on the definition; SB-2 uses it in M7).

6. **Search** (SR-1, SR-2): one search box covering every entry, chapter text, and saved briefs (briefs come in M7; index the collection now so they appear when they exist), in English and Chinese; filters by section, status, provenance, family, place, tag, and year range; under 200 ms with 5,000 entries (test it).

7. **Tests**: unit for every pure function (culture and place lookups by year, romanization checker with valid and invalid Pinyin and Jyutping samples, knowledge queries against the fixture's two facts including H5 learning one in 2060, outline grid data, reorder logic, search filters); integration through the repository and IPC for every new collection; e2e: create and edit each entity type through the interface (T-4 coverage for these sections), reorder a chapter by drag, type `@` to link, hover a Chinese name and see the meaning, mark a question decided, close the app and answer the stop prompt, relaunch and see the note; the T-5 field walk must now cover every new entity's manifest.

Constraints: no em dashes anywhere; `fixtures/expected/` untouched; pure logic in `src/shared/domain/`; test names start with the criterion ID. Report at the end: `npm test` tail, criterion to test names table, files changed, anything not done with the reason.
