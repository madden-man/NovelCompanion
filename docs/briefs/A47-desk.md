This is the screen Tommy writes against. He said "creatively handling each page in such a way that it's both editable and usable during writing sessions": the desk must read like a brief and edit like a form without ever looking like one. Nothing opens a dialog, nothing leaves the screen, and the order of sections (`SB-1`, `SB-2`: scene note, characters, events window, culture, backstory, more) stays.

Deliverables:

1. **The heading** is an `Editable` text over the chapter's title; the story year and the point of view beside it are `Editable` year and select over the writing strand (`strands.ts` builds the patch, one step labelled "Story year of <chapter>" or "Point of view of <chapter>"). Changing the year here moves the top bar's story year the way the Writing picker does.
2. **Tiles.** Each character tile edits name, Chinese name, birth, death (`Editable` text and date), In the story and Description (the A44 box in place of the read-only Markdown view) without expanding into a form: the collapsed tile shows name, Chinese name and age as fields; the expanded tile shows the rest. The family line becomes a link to the tree. The three latest moments are `Editable` text each (year and what happens), and "Add a moment in <story year>" creates one for that person at the story year and opens its text. "Their page" and "Their backstory" stay.
3. **In this scene** is a chip row over the writing strand's `characterIds` (`Editable` chips with the character picker; "Add new..." creates a character with that name and adds it), one step per add or remove labelled "Added <name> to <chapter>". The tiles follow the chips at once.
4. **World events strip** gets "Add an event in <story year>" at its end: creates an event at the story year (category: the first category item, status from the statuses list's first item of kind planned if any, else canon) and opens its title as a field on the strip. Event titles on the strip are `Editable` text.
5. **Cultural context**: each line is `Editable` text in place (click opens the field, not the page; a small "Open" link beside it opens the page). The gap button keeps "Ask Claude" and gains "Write it yourself" beside it: creates a cultural line in that community for the story year's unit and the age group `all`, and opens its text as a field.
6. **Backstory rows** edit year and what happens in place; a link opens the moment page.
7. **More**: every item shows its title (no raw IDs anywhere on the screen, grep for `_id` in the templates), and has Remove and Keep (`SB-9`) before any brief is generated; `start.tsx`'s selection preview has the same. The brief selection in `brief.tsx` shows titles, not IDs, in the pending list.
8. **Re-import** (`IP-18`): when the chapter has a `manuscriptSource`, a "Re-import" button at the foot of the desk calls `manuscript.reimport` and shows the diff the Manuscript tab shows (reuse its component if it stands alone, else the smallest copy) with Apply, which is what the importer path already does.
9. **IP-6 on Sessions**: Regenerate on a brief whose text was edited says "This replaces your edits" on the button's line, and the regenerate is one undo step (a `run` op in `undoCommands.ts` that puts the previous text back; the handler in `sessionHandlers.ts` 280 returns the previous text so the renderer can record it). "New log entry" on Sessions creates a session log row by hand (date today, chapter the one being written) and opens it under its row.
10. **Tests** in `session.spec.ts`, each prefixed with its criterion: the heading renames the chapter and Undo renames it back; the year and point of view write the strand; a tile edits name, birth and role in place and the age follows the birth; Add a moment lands at the story year and the tile shows it; the chip row adds and removes a character and the tiles follow; Add an event makes an event at the story year with a title typed in place; Write it yourself makes a cultural line in the community at the unit and age group all; More shows no raw IDs and Remove and Keep work before generating; Re-import shows the diff and applies it (the fixture's Word chapter); Regenerate warns and undoes; New log entry opens under its row; NF-2 every control by keyboard in both themes at 1200x800 and 390 wide.
