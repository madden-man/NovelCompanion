# A39 world brief: the World events grid (SM-8)

Read `docs/briefs/A39-common.md` first. You own the files listed there under `a39-world`. SPEC: SM-8, with WE-1 to WE-3 (events and eras, the editable category list), TL-6 only for the computations the grid reuses (`src/shared/domain/timeline.ts`: the year scale, era bands, strands, the story year), A33 (the five-year events window), ED-1 (the inspector editor), NF-1 (a 5,000-entry bible), NF-2 (keyboard and contrast).

Tommy's words: "world events, it should all be graphically viewable", and the original world tracker sheet: years across the top, one row per category, many rows of markers.

Deliverables:

1. **SM-8 the grid replaces the chart.** The Timeline screen (rail button "Timeline" for now; wave 2 renames it "World events") becomes one grid. Columns are years, 2025 to 2100 by default; a zoom control picks pixels per year (whole span fits the panel, default, and close) and the grid scrolls sideways past the panel. Rows are the subcategories of the `eventCategories` list, one row each, grouped under their parent category as a collapsible heading, in the list's order (the East Winds list came from the sheet in the sheet's order; the fixture has Technology with Robotics and Energy, and so on). Events with a top-level category and no subcategory sit in a row of their own directly under that heading. A closed heading shows its rows' markers merged onto the heading line. The year header is sticky at the top and the row labels are sticky at the left; the labels use the primary text token (never muted).
2. **Markers.** An event is a marker in its row at its start year, a bar to its end year when it has one, larger when `formative`. Its title shows on hover (a tooltip) and as a direct label to the right when the next marker in the row leaves room. The era band runs under the year header (one segment per era state with its name, reusing the TL-6 era computation), the story year is a vertical line across every row, and the chapter being written (the Writing picker's chapter) is a vertical band over its primary strand's years.
3. **Edit from the grid.** Clicking an empty spot in a row opens a small form at that spot: title, year (filled from the click), end year, formative; Save writes a world event through the write path (`entry.create`, one undo step) with that category and opens it in the screen's inspector (`EmbeddedEditor`, as the old chart marks did). Clicking a marker opens its event in the inspector. A marker drags along its row to change its start year (its end year moves with it when it has one), with the year shown live while dragging; the drop writes one `entry.update` (one undo step); the Left and Right arrows do the same from the keyboard, Shift with the arrow for the end year. Markers are focusable buttons in reading order (row by row, year by year).
4. **Filter and the world around the story year.** A region filter (toggle chips with `aria-pressed`, "All regions" plus every region found on the events) narrows the markers. Above the grid, a short panel "The world around <story year>" lists the events from three years before the story year to two years after it (titles only, a title opens the event in the inspector), so the writer sees the window A33 uses without scrolling the grid; the grid itself opens scrolled so the story year is in view.
5. **Pure logic in `src/shared/domain/worldGrid.ts`**: row building from the category list (groups, rows, merged rows for closed groups), marker placement (x from year and scale, bar widths, label room), hit testing from a pointer position to a row and year, drag arithmetic (pixels to years, clamped to the span), the window around the story year. Unit tests for each, including a 5,000-event bible laid out within the NF-1 budget.
6. **Tests.** `tests/e2e/timeline.spec.ts` is yours end to end. Keep the TM rows that still hold (story time moves the line) and replace the TL-6 chart rows and the KB-4 rows with SM-8 rows: the fixture's categories appear as rows under their groups in list order; the fixture's events sit at their years (read the marker's year from a data attribute); a group collapses and its markers merge; a click on an empty spot creates an event at that row and year through the write path and the inspector shows it, and Undo removes it; dragging a marker changes its year and Undo restores it, and so does ArrowRight; the region chips narrow the markers; the story year line and the writing band are where the story time says; "The world around" lists the five-year window and opens an event; the whole screen by keyboard (NF-2) with no muted labels (contrast per `tests/helpers/contrast.ts`).

Not yours: the Backstory grid (wave 2 reuses your year axis and scale, so keep `worldGrid.ts` free of event-specific types where the axis is concerned), the rail name, the schema. `reality` and `placeIds` are leaving world events on the model builder's branch; do not show or filter by them. Era states are edited in Cultural data in wave 2; the grid only draws the band.
