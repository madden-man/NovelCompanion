# A39 outline brief: scenes with years beside chapter bars (SM-5, SM-7)

Read `docs/briefs/A39-common.md` first. You own the files listed there under `a39-outline`. SPEC: SM-5, SM-7, PL-5 (the text editing rules, versions, autosave, Shift+Up and Shift+Down, all of which stay), A37 (`breaks`: chapter ID to the line its section starts on, which stays as the storage), TM-4 (the Writing picker follows the story year), NF-2.

Tommy's words: "the outline should have two connected panels: the scenes on the left with their year, and vertical draggable chapter bars on the right", and "separate field per scene for year".

History you will reuse: commit 4b2ce4a `src/renderer/src/workspace/outline/Scenes.tsx` holds a working chapter track beside the text (bars dragged among the scenes, resize from the edges, keyboard moves, tests in `plot.spec.ts` at that commit). It was built on `ranges`; you bring the track back on `breaks`. Read that commit's `Scenes.tsx` and `plot.spec.ts` with `git show 4b2ce4a:<path>` before you start.

Deliverables:

1. **SM-5 years per scene.** The `meta` scene outline document and every version gain `years`: a map from scene line index (the top-level bullet's line in the text, the same indexing `breaks` uses) to a year. `sceneOutline.set` carries it; the pure edit helpers in `src/shared/domain/sceneOutline.ts` keep `years` in step with the text the way they keep `breaks` (a line inserted above shifts the indices, a deleted scene drops its year, a split or join moves it, a reorder of chapters carries the years with their lines). A scene without a year inherits the nearest scene above with one, else its chapter's primary strand year; the gutter shows the year beside every scene as a small field in the chapter's colour, muted and dashed when inherited, and typing a year in it sets that scene's own year (clearing it returns to inheritance). `ranges` and everything that read it are removed from the document, the schema, the IPC and the versions (an old version with `ranges` still opens: ignore the field). Versions store `years` and a restore brings them back.
2. **SM-7 the two panels.** Left, the scene text as today (bullets, indented lines as notes on the scene above, Enter continues a bullet, Cmd+] and Cmd+[ indent, autosave to Saved, versions list and preview). The chapter headings inside the text (A37) go: the text is scenes only. Right, a track with one vertical bar per chapter in reading order, as tall as the lines of its section (from its break to the next chapter's break, or the end), in the chapter's colour, titled with the chapter's name and its part; a scene count under the title. The two panels scroll as one element, so a bar is always beside its scenes, and the gutter of every scene carries its chapter's colour as a stripe. Pulling a bar's top or bottom edge moves that chapter's break (the neighbour's section grows or shrinks; a section never empties below one scene); dragging a bar past a neighbour swaps the two chapters (their reading order and their scene sections move together, through `plot.reorder` and a `sceneOutline.set` in one undo step, as A37 did for a reorder); from the keyboard, Up and Down on a focused bar swap it with its neighbour and Shift+Up and Shift+Down move its end edge a scene, Alt+Up and Alt+Down its start edge. A chapter's year and point of view are still edited where PL-5 put them (the story year field beside the chapter and the Writing picker); the chapter's name is edited by its `InlineName` on the bar. The first scene's inherited year is the chapter's primary strand year, so a change of either shows at once.
3. **The year grid goes.** `OutlineView.tsx` loses the Scenes and Year grid toggle and the year grid (PL-2, withdrawn by SM-12); the Outline opens on the two panels, nothing else. Leave the arcs panel in `OutlineView.tsx` alone (the model builder removes it); if the toggle and the grid are tangled with it, remove only your part and say so. `outline/Strands.tsx` and `ChapterPage.tsx` are not yours.
4. **Pure logic** stays in `src/shared/domain/sceneOutline.ts`: `years` maintenance for every text edit, inheritance (`yearOf(lineIndex)`), the bar geometry from `breaks` and the line count, the edge-move and swap arithmetic with their limits, and what one swap or edge move writes. Unit tests for each in `tests/unit/scene-outline.test.ts`, on the A37 cases plus: a year set on a scene survives an insert above it, a deleted scene's year goes, a split scene keeps its year on the first half, a swap carries years with their scenes, inheritance across a chapter boundary stops at the chapter's year.
5. **Tests.** `tests/e2e/plot.spec.ts` is yours: keep the PL-5 rows that still hold (autosave, gutter numbers, versions, text survives leaving the screen, the story year field), replace the A37 heading rows and the year grid rows, and add SM-5 and SM-7 rows: a year typed in the gutter is stored over IPC in `years` and shown beside the scene, the scene below inherits it in the muted style and stops inheriting when it gets its own, the first scene shows the chapter's year; the bars match the fixture's chapters and sections; pulling a bar's bottom edge a scene moves the break and the neighbour shrinks, Undo restores it; dragging a bar past the next bar swaps the chapters, the text sections move with them and the reading numbers change, one Undo restores both; the keyboard does the same from a focused bar; the panels scroll together (scroll the container and assert a bar's top stays beside its first scene); a restore of a version brings back its years; the Outline by keyboard (NF-2). Integration rows in `tests/integration/outline.test.ts`: `years` round-trips, is versioned and restored, `ranges` no longer stored, an old document with `ranges` still opens.

Not yours: the chapter page, strands, arcs, the schema of chapters, the rail name.
