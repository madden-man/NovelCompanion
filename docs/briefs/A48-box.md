# A48 box brief: branch `a48-box` (CR-6, the renderer side of CR-9, the Route A tests of CR-10)

Read `docs/briefs/A48-common.md` first, then `docs/briefs/A48-wave1-api.md` (what `a48-router` and `a48-fast` built and how to call it). You change the Cmd+K box so Enter routes before anything is sent, Route A facts file from the box without Claude, and routes B and C send with hints.

## What exists

- `src/renderer/src/workspace/capture.tsx`: `CaptureBox` (phases edit, running, review; `send`, `file`, `saveAsQuestion`; proposal cards fetched through `proposal.diff` and drawn with `ProposalDiff` from `review.tsx`); `capture.css`. `src/shared/domain/capture.ts`: `fileCapture` (approves ticked proposals as one undo step) and `captureQuestionData` (KB-7).
- `workspace/data.tsx`: `useEntries(collection)` and `useList(listId)` give the store's live documents; `workspace/bibleSettings.ts` `useBibleSettings()` gives `numbers.timelineEnd`. `workspace/storyTime.ts` `useStoryTime` gives the year, chapter and strand.
- `src/shared/domain/captureRouter/`: `buildIndex`, `routeCapture`, the lexicon with cue labels, `localSourceNote`, `CaptureHint`.
- `src/shared/undoCommands.ts`: `listAddCommand`; `src/shared/domain/undoRunner.ts`: `Op` and `UndoCommand`; `fileCapture` shows how a filing becomes one step.
- Tests: `tests/e2e/capture.spec.ts` (KB-6 and KB-7 rows against the fake), `tests/integration/capture.test.ts`, `tests/unit/capture.test.ts`.

## Build

1. On Enter (CR-6): build the index from the store (characters, places, culturalEntries, moments, glossaryTerms, the lists, `timelineEnd`), run `routeCapture` with the story year, the writing strand's community and no hints, and move to a new phase `routed`. Keep the KB-7 path: with no key, the text still becomes an open question; with a key, Route A facts never need it, so a bible with no key can still file Route A facts (say so in a line of the box).
2. The routed view (CR-6, CR-9): one card per fact, in order. A Route A card shows the chips for its section (Section always; Community, Age group, Years for cultural data; Character, Year for a moment; Category, Year for a world event; Community for a place), the drafts as the ED-7 diff (build a `ProposalView` from the draft the way `review.tsx` expects, or add a small `DraftDiff` that draws created fields the same way), a tick (ticked by default), and the cue labels in small text ("a group starts to say or do something, a demonym plus a place"). A chip is a select or a year field; a change reruns the router with a hint for that fact and redraws the card; a missing required field is a highlighted chip that must be filled before the card can be ticked. A Route B or C card shows its chips (read-only once sent) and streams its proposals exactly as the review phase does today.
3. Sending (CR-9): when any fact is on Route B or C, call `claude.capture` with the text, the story time and the hints (one per fact that was changed or forced; facts on Route A carry `route: 'local'` so main skips them), then listen to the run(s) as today. When every fact is Route A, call nothing.
4. File it (Cmd+Enter): ticked Route A drafts write through `entry.create` and `lists.addItem` (new items first, `ref` values replaced by the minted ID, as `fileCapture` does for `listAdd`), with `sourceNote: localSourceNote(today)` and status idea (`useDefaultStatus`); ticked Route B and C proposals approve through `fileCapture`. All of it is one undo step (ED-3), labelled "Filed a capture: ..." with the titles. Put the local writing in `src/shared/domain/capture.ts` as `fileLocalDrafts(invoke, drafts, capturedOn, status)` returning the same `Filing` shape, and merge the two commands. Nothing is written before File it.
5. Ask Claude instead on a Route A card forces `route: 'full'` for that fact and sends. Review later saves the whole text as the KB-7 open question. Escape closes and writes nothing. Unticked Route A drafts are dropped; unticked proposals are rejected as today.
6. The Filed list links every entry written, local or approved. The provenance line on a local entry reads "From a capture on <date>, filed without Claude."
7. Tests (CR-10): `tests/unit/capture.test.ts` or `tests/integration/capture.test.ts`: `fileLocalDrafts` writes a cultural line with the local source note and the default idea status, mints the list item first, and undoes as one step with the approved proposals; nothing is written before it is called. `tests/e2e/capture.spec.ts`: "CR-6 Enter on a clear cultural line shows chips and a diff and sends nothing; Cmd+Enter files it and the Filed list links it" (use a line the fixture bible resolves: look at the fixture's communities, characters and places in `fixtures/novel/`), "CR-6 changing the Section chip re-routes", "CR-6 a missing community is a highlighted chip and File it waits for it", "CR-9 a two-sentence capture shows two cards; the Route A one files locally and the Route B one streams from the recording", "CR-6 Cmd+Z undoes a mixed filing in one step", "CR-6 Ask Claude instead sends the fact on Route C". Keep every KB-6 and KB-7 row that still holds; change the ones Enter's new behaviour breaks and say which.

Keep the box a reading surface (A47): chips sit in one row under the card title, the diff keeps the ED-7 table, nothing moves when a chip opens.
