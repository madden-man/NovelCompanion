Tommy's words: "drop arcs in favor of backstory grid, yes backstory is per character". The original Sullivan and 李 sheets had one dated moment per row per character with "relevant world events" and "how the POV character experiences this"; the grid is those sheets across one year axis.

Deliverables:

1. **SM-9 the grid.** Years left to right on the same axis and zoom as World events (reuse `worldGrid.ts` for the axis, the sticky year header, the era band, the story year line and the writing band; do not duplicate that logic). One row per character, grouped under a heading per family in the tree's order (`src/shared/domain/tree.ts` gives the layout order; characters with no family come last under "No family"), the row label the character's name (primary text token) with their Chinese name. In each row a lifespan bar from birth to death (or to the end of the axis when alive, dashed past the story year) in the family colour, and a marker per moment at its year (a bar to `endYear` when set), the title on hover and as a direct label when there is room. The family headings collapse. A row's label is a button that picks the person.
2. **The moments table.** Below the grid, the picked person's moments in year order as an editable table: Year, Age (computed, read-only, TL-3), What happens (`title`), How they experience it (`body`), Relevant world events (`worldContext`, with a picker beside it that lists the world events of that year plus or minus one and inserts a title on click), and a Delete that trashes the row with Undo. Each cell saves through the write path on blur or Enter (one `entry.update` per cell, one undo step); a new row comes from "Add a moment" (year prefilled with the story year) or from a click on an empty spot in the person's grid row at that year, through `entry.create`, and gets focus on What happens. A marker drags along its row to change the year (one undo step; Left and Right arrows do the same from a focused marker). A marker click picks the person and highlights that row in the table.
3. **Pure logic in `src/shared/domain/backstoryGrid.ts`**: row building (families in tree order, people in each, lifespan spans from the StoryDates with the unknown cases of TL-2), marker placement and label room through `worldGrid.ts`, hit testing to a person and year, the events-of-a-year list for the picker, age per row. Unit tests on the fixture novel: the rows for the fixture's families, Margaret Hale's lifespan 1958 to 2041, an alive character's open bar, the moments the fixture holds, a 5,000-moment bible laid out within the NF-1 budget.
4. **Wiring.** The rail button "Backstory" and the view kind `backstory` exist from the shell builder's placeholder (`backstory/Placeholder.tsx`); do not create that file.
