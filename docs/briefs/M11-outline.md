Read `docs/briefs/M11-common.md` first. You own `src/renderer/src/workspace/plot.tsx` (split it into `src/renderer/src/workspace/outline/*.tsx` if that helps), `src/shared/domain/outline.ts`, the `outline.*` and `plot.*` channels in `src/shared/ipc.ts` and their handlers in `src/main/ipc/bibleHandlers.ts`, `tests/e2e/plot.spec.ts` and the unit tests of the outline domain. SPEC: PL-1 to PL-4, section 4 rows for Book, Part, Arc, Chapter, PlotBeat, ED-1 to ED-5 for how entries are edited.

Tommy's words: "outline (just one is fine, make it easy to move around and don't create separate views for parts/arc/strands/whatever)". The Outline is the only place he should need for the story's structure.

Deliverables:

1. **Create in place.** A "New book" control on the outline; "New part" on each book heading; "New chapter" on each part heading (it takes a title, creates the chapter with one primary strand at the story year, and opens its page); "New beat" on the chapter page and on each arc. Each new entry goes through the same write path (`entry.create` via the `useUndo` command helpers the editors already use) and is one undo step.
2. **Rename and delete in place.** Book, part and chapter titles edit inline (click or Enter on the heading, Escape cancels, blur saves) through the ED-1 editor path; delete follows ED-5 (Move to trash with the linked-from count) from the heading's menu or the inspector.
3. **Move.** Drag stays. Add keyboard moves: with a chapter row focused, Alt+Up and Alt+Down reorder within the part, and a "Move to part" select on the chapter page; parts reorder within a book the same way (add `plot.reorder` support for parts and books if missing). Every move is one undo step (PL-3). Reading order and story-year order stay separate.
4. **The chapter page edits everything.** In the outline inspector the chapter page edits: title, part, strands (label, primary flag, start year or range, point of view, characters present in order, places), arcs, themes, facts in play, written flag; beats in reading order with move up and down (exists) and New beat. Use `EmbeddedEditor` and the field manifests rather than hand-made forms where the manifest covers it; the strands list needs a purpose-made editor if the generic one is clumsy. Arcs: an "Arcs" disclosure on the outline lists arcs with owner and scope, lets me add one and rename it, and clicking one highlights its span (exists) and lists its beats.
5. **Glance.** Each part heading shows its chapter count; each chapter row shows its strand years and point of view (existing marks), and the row text is the chapter title with its reading number.
6. **Tests.** `tests/e2e/plot.spec.ts`: KB-3 new book, part, chapter and beat from the outline; KB-3 rename a chapter inline and undo it; KB-3 Alt+Down moves a chapter and the reading order persists after reload; KB-3 the chapter page edits a strand's point of view and characters present and the change shows in the row; KB-3 Move to part. Unit tests for any new pure logic in `src/shared/domain/outline.ts`; integration tests for new or changed `plot.*` handlers. The existing PL tests keep passing.
