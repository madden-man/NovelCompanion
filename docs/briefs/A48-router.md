# A48 router brief: branch `a48-router` (CR-1 to CR-5, the fixture of CR-10)

Read `docs/briefs/A48-common.md` first. You build the pure router under `src/shared/domain/captureRouter/`, replacing the placeholder `index.ts` and keeping every export of `types.ts` (add to it only at the end, and say so).

## What exists

- `docs/briefs/A48-router-draft.js`: the browser draft of the rules from the design page, with the real East Winds seed data at the top. It is the reference for every rule below; port it, do not copy it. Its seed data becomes the East Winds slice of the fixture (`fixtures/capture/eastwinds-slice.json`: the lists `communities`, `eventCategories` (the top level and the subtrees the draft names), `ageGroups`, `languages`; the characters, places, cultural lines, moments and glossary terms the draft lists, as plain documents in the shape of the schema).
- The schema: `src/shared/schema/*` (`characters.names.full` and `.chinese`, `places.communityId` and `.parentId`, `culturalEntries` with `communityId`, `startYear`, `endYear`, `demographic`, `moments` with `characterId`, `year`, `endYear`, `worldEvents` with `start`, `end`, `categoryId`, `regions`, `glossaryTerms` with `characters`, `romanization`, `language`, `meaning`, `usageNote`, `openQuestions` with `question`, `tags`). Lists: `src/shared/schema/lists.ts` and `src/main/db/defaultLists.ts` (the `ageGroups` items carry `extra.from` and `extra.to`).
- The tokenizer the search index uses (`src/main/search/tokenize.ts`): `foldLatin` is the right way to compare Latin names, and CJK matching is by substring. You may import nothing from `src/main`; copy the one function you need into the router with a comment saying where it came from, or move it to `src/shared/` if that is cleaner (say so).

## Build

1. `buildIndex(input: RouterIndexInput): RouterIndex`. Characters: aliases are the full name, the first name (and the surname-first first name for a Chinese name such as "Li Qin" giving "qin"), the Chinese name and its given-name part, plus the lexicon's aliases by ID; lowercase and folded. Places: the name and the lexicon's aliases, with `communityId`. Communities: the label and the lexicon's aliases and demonyms by item ID; items the lexicon lacks match by label alone. Categories: the label and the lexicon's cue words by ID. Age groups: the list's items with the lexicon's words by ID (`all` is never matched; it is the default). Cultural lines, moments, glossary as the types say. Deleted entries (`deletedAt`) are left out.
2. `lexicon.ts`: every cue of CR-4 with its weight and its plain label; the aliases and demonyms per community ID; the cue words per category ID; the words per age group ID; the year grammar; the life verbs; the stop words. One exported object, typed, so the Settings screen can show it later and the box can show cue labels.
3. `routeCapture(text, index, options): CaptureRouting` (CR-1), built from `parse`, `resolve`, `score`, `pickCommunity`, `draftsFor` and `decide`, each exported for the tests:
   - Split into sentences (CR-2): on `.`, `!`, `?` followed by whitespace and a capital or a CJK character; a lone line break also splits. A fact with no year of its own takes the start year of the fact before it, with source "the year of the sentence before".
   - Years per CR-2. The draft's `parse` is the reference. An open end on a cultural line stores `index.timelineEnd`; a moment stores `endYear: null`.
   - Resolve per CR-3. Match whole words (a match may end at an apostrophe, so "Sydney's" matches "sydney"). Prefer the longest alias per entity; a candidate whose matched alias sits inside another candidate's longer match is dropped (so "Sydney's husband (unnamed)" wins over "Sydney Sullivan", and "Siyuan's temple" is a place with no character). Unknown names: a capitalised word or run of words mid-sentence that matches nothing and is not a stop word, taking a following "district", "neighborhood", "street", "avenue", "building", "bridge" or "park" with it.
   - Community candidates: a place's community first, then alias matches, then the demonym rule (a demonym plus a place in California also offers the diaspora community, after the place's community; a demonym alone maps to its national community), then `options.povCommunityId` marked as the writing strand (weak). Deduplicate, keep order.
   - Score per CR-4; the draft's `LEX` and the adjustments after it are the reference (the question-mark rule, the group-subject discount, the character-subject discount, the place-as-subject cue). Record the cue labels per section in `cues`.
   - Drafts per section, in the shape of main's `ProposalDraft` (`op: 'create'`, `collection`, `data`; `op: 'listAdd'` for a new list item with `listId`, `label`, `ref`, and the `ref` used as the value in the draft that needs it). Cultural data: `title` (the sentence with its year phrase removed, capitalised, cut at 90 characters), `communityId`, `demographic` (the matched age group or `all`), `startYear`, `endYear`, `body` (the sentence), `status: 'idea'`. Moment: one per character, `characterId`, `year`, `endYear`, title "Lives in <place>" for a move or "lives in", else the sentence title, `body`. World event: `title`, `start`, `end`, `categoryId` (only when exactly one category matched; else missing), `regions` from matched places, `body`. Place: `title` (the unknown name or the matched place), `parentId` of a second matched place, `communityId`, `body`. Glossary: `characters` (the CJK run or the quoted word), `language` (`mandarin` for CJK else `english`), `usageNote` (the sentence). Open question: `title`, `question`, `tags: ['capture']`. Character: `names.full` and `title` from the unknown name, `body`. The source note is not set here: the box and main add it (`localSourceNote` for Route A, the capture's note for B and C).
   - `missing` and `asks`: cultural data needs a community (askable); a moment needs a character (askable); a world event needs a category (not askable, a judgment: Route B); a place and a character need a name (askable); a glossary term needs a term (askable).
   - `reasons` (judgment flags): an unknown name outside the place and character sections, more than one character for a moment, a quoted word beside a moment ("goes by", "calls", a nickname) that may also be a glossary term, a category to choose, "is a" or "works as" on a character (might be a role, not a moment), and "section only N% sure" when the share is under the local threshold and nothing else is flagged.
   - Decide per CR-5 with `options.thresholds ?? DEFAULT_THRESHOLDS`. Apply `options.hints` before deciding: a hint's `section` replaces the top section (and a forced section on a single sentence with no reasons goes to Route B rather than C); `communityId`, `characterId`, `categoryId`, `demographic`, `startYear`, `endYear` replace the resolved values and clear the matching `missing` entry; `route` forces the route.
4. Tests (`tests/unit/captureRouter.test.ts`, more files if long): one test per criterion rule above, titles starting with the criterion ID; and the fixture run: `fixtures/capture/examples.json` holds the twelve lines of the design page with their expected route, section, community, years, age group and (for moments) the character names, and one test runs them all against the slice and prints a table of mismatches. The expected values are on the design page's table and in the draft; the architect verified them on 2026-10-22: 1 A cultural san-francisco 2065 to timeline end everyone; 2 A cultural jinan-shandong 2070 to end, ages 0 to 18; 3 A moment Mark Sullivan 2061 "Lives in Seattle"; 4 A open question; 5 A cultural china-national 2068 to end; 6 B world event 2068, category to choose (Transportation or Pacific States Compact); 7 A cultural pure-land-buddhism 2070 to end; 8 B moments for Dawn Li and Mark Sullivan (LiNa is the object and may be dropped or kept; say what you chose); 9 B moment for Wang'an plus a glossary term "Wanda"; 10 B place "Mission district", unknown name; 11 B moment or role on "Sydney's husband (unnamed)"; 12 three facts: "In 2063 the second crash hits." (world event, category to choose, 2063), "Qin's drone factory loses its export license" (inherits 2063; the place maps to jinan-shandong; the sentence names no character, so it is cultural data or a world event, Route B either way), "Xuan takes a Party job in Beijing, and the Li family apartment is sold." (moment for Li Xuan, 2063, Route B because of the second clause). Where your port disagrees with these, make the rule better and record the difference in the report rather than bending a rule to a number.

Keep the router free of React, Electron and MongoDB (ARCHITECTURE rule 1). Keep it fast: it runs on every Enter in the box on a bible with a few hundred entries; build the index once per call and avoid regular expressions built inside loops.
